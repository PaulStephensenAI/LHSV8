Professional Focus & Role
Founder & Architect: Paul Stephensen is the founder of Lavender Hill Studio and principal architect of sovereign digital workspaces.
Field of Expertise: AI Ethicist developing modular, schema driven AI applications that respect human autonomy and mitigate cognitive fatigue.
Core Research Focus: Intersections of moral philosophy, cognitive ergonomics, agentic artificial intelligence, and non extractive data governance.

The Lavender Hill Studio Vision
Paul Stephensen established Lavender Hill Studio around three core principles:

1. Human Centered AI Engineering:
- Technology must adapt to the human rhythm rather than forcing humans to adapt to artificial speed.
- AI tools must operate transparently, predictably, and without deceptive engagement loops or attention harvesting.

2. Data Dignity & Ownership:
- Personal and organizational data belong exclusively to the individual or team that creates them.
- Software should default to zero telemetry and local storage unless explicit, revocable cloud sync is requested.

3. Dual Path Hardware Sovereignty:
Championing a genuine choice between:
a) Local Sovereign Software: Running 100% offline on personal Windows 11 laptops and Samsung Galaxy tablets.
b) Clean Cloud Deployments: Deployed directly to client owned Vercel cloud environments with zero recurring SaaS subscriptions managed by the studio.
